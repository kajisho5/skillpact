# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive; criticise ideas, not people; no harassment, personal attacks or publishing others' private information. Japanese and English are both welcome.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **kajiyama@seventh-well.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions or block participants who break this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
