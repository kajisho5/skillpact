# Names, trademarks and badges

- **SEVENTHWELL** is the name of the publisher of SkillPact. Do not use it in a way that suggests SEVENTHWELL endorses, reviewed or certified your product or skill.
- **SkillPact** may be used to describe things that follow the specification, e.g. "SkillPact contract", "supports SkillPact 0.8", "SkillPact validator". Do not use it as the name of a different or modified format; if you change the format, call it something else and say it is derived from SkillPact.
- No registered trademark is claimed at this time; these rules state how we ask the names to be used.

## Badges
- Show an L1, L2 or L3 badge only for the level that `skillpact conformance` or the GitHub Action actually reported for the published version of your skill.
- Re-check after every change; remove or downgrade the badge if the level drops.
- Link the badge to the SkillPact site or to your CI result.
- Do not alter the badge to show a level you did not reach, and do not imply that a level means "safe" (spec §16.1).
- Badge images are under CC BY 4.0 like the specification text.
